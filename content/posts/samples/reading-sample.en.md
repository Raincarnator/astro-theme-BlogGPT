## Color before words

A gradient image can give a page a quiet beginning. Light areas leave room to breathe, darker areas provide a visual center, and fine grain adds texture to smooth transitions.

Words follow the color, unfolding along the natural line width. Some paragraphs suit short, brisk sentences; some thoughts ask for a longer pause, using a longer sentence to connect what comes before and after. Headings, paragraphs and space work together to give reading its rhythm.

> Leave the focus to content and the space to reading.

## Another color

![Blue and cyan watercolor](/images/illustrations/blue-flow.webp)

An illustration is a pause between paragraphs. Your eye moves from words to color, then returns to the next passage, giving the page another change of pace.

## Leave some space

An article can be brief or unfold gradually. Keep related ideas together and give each paragraph a clear focus to create a comfortable space for reading.
